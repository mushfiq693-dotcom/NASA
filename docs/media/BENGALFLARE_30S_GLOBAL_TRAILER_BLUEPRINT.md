## 🎯 What Makes This Video "International Level" (Judges' Scoring Secrets)

1. **No Wasted Seconds on Greetings:**
   - ❌ Never start with: *"Hello judges, my name is Mushfiq and this is our project..."* (Wastes 8 seconds; judges immediately skip).
   - ✅ Hook immediately with an intriguing global paradox (*"For 24 years, NASA satellites watched our planet burn..."*).
2. **Kinetic Typography (Silent-Viewer Proof):**
   - 80% of judges watch submissions in crowded evaluation halls or on laptops with low audio.
   - Large, punchy 2-3 word phrases on screen ensure the entire core message is delivered even on mute!
3. **Show Real Working Product, Not Fakes:**
   - Global judges despise pure slide decks or generic stock footage. 
   - 70% of the screen time shows **actual smooth 60 FPS recordings of `bengalflare.vercel.app`**:
     - 3D Globe with orbit satellites.
     - Rothermel & Huygens threat cone expanding.
     - Smart ML Fire Attribution badge (`🌾 96% Human-Made`).
     - Telegram notification delivery.
4. **Cinematic Sound Design (SFX):**
   - Don't just slap royalty-free corporate ukulele/corporate whistle music.
   - Use a modern hybrid cinematic trailer track (e.g. Hans Zimmer / Tenet style ticking clock + sub-bass drops + digital interface chirps).
